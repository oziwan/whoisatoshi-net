The Strongest Candidates

SATOSHI NAKAMOTO
Bitcoin Creator
2008–2011

HAL FINNEY

It didn't move at all
1956–2014

Strong Evidence:

Satoshi conducted the first Bitcoin transaction (3 January 2009).

The closest match in the writing style analysis. His neighbor's name is Dorian Nakamoto.

Active member of the cypherpunk community; PGP expert.

Counter Argument:

An ALS patient, diagnosed in 2009, underwent cryopreservation in 2014.

NICK SZABO

Bit Gold + Smart Contract
b. 1964

Strong Evidence:

Bit Gold (1998), Bitcoin's closest technical predecessor.

NYT: the most compelling evidence points to Szabo. The blog's silence following the whitepaper publication is noteworthy.

Smart contracts are based on the Bitcoin protocol.

Counter Argument:

He rejected the possibility of being Satoshi.

ADAM BACK

Hashcash / PoW inventor
b. 1965

Strong Evidence:

The only name Satoshi mentioned in the whitepaper.

Hashcash is the basis of Bitcoin's PoW mechanism. Leading expert in cryptography and active in Bitcoin's early development.

Counter Argument:

He has an unassuming, transparent career as CEO of Blockstream.

LEN SASSAMAN

PGP Developer
1980–2011

Suspicious Evidence:

Satoshi disappeared months after his last forum message.

Close Cypherpunk collaboration with Hal Finney. Deep expertise in PGP and cryptography; academic work on anonymous online communication.

Counter-argument:

Verification is impossible due to his death; the direct evidence is weak.
